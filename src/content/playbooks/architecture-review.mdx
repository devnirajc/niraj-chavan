---
title: How I review an architecture
summary: Follow the data and the change, not the diagram — then ask what breaks first under growth.
draft: true
order: 4
steps:
  - title: Start from the use cases
    detail: Which journeys matter most, and what are their latency, availability and security needs? The architecture exists to serve those.
  - title: Trace the data
    detail: Where is it created, who owns it, where is it cached, and how does it get invalidated? Most front-end bugs live in the gaps here.
  - title: Trace a change
    detail: Pick a likely next feature and walk it through. How many modules, teams and deploys does it touch? That number is the architecture's real cost.
  - title: Find the boundaries
    detail: Are module and team boundaries in the same place? Shared code needs an owner, a version and a release process.
  - title: Ask what fails first
    detail: Ten times the users, the data, the team. What breaks — bundle size, build time, coordination, an API?
  - title: Write it down
    detail: Decisions recorded with their context and the options rejected, so the reasoning survives the people who made it.
---

A good architecture makes the common change cheap. Reviewing one is mostly a matter of finding out what the common change is.
