Scope Retail builds supply-chain software for large retailers. At Xpanxion I designed and built the UI of an order management system we built for them in Angular, from scratch. It is one admin application where retail teams manage orders, inventory and the product catalogue, control who can do what through users and roles, and follow the business on real-time dashboards and reports. This was client work, so there is no public demo or repository. The photo above is illustrative.

- **Client:** Scope Retail, supply-chain software for large retailers
- **Product:** a greenfield order management system covering orders, inventory, catalogue, users and reporting
- **Users:** retail teams, including the operations team, who adopted its dashboards as their primary daily tool
- **My role:** Senior UI Engineer at Xpanxion. I designed and built the full UI for inventory oversight and order management, including its real-time dashboards, and mentored two junior engineers on the team
- **When:** between 2017 and 2019, during my time at Xpanxion (now UST Global)

## The business problem

Before this system, the operations team tracked inventory and orders in **manual spreadsheets**. That had two costs:

- **Manual data entry produced errors.** Orders and stock were keyed in by hand, and errors crept in.
- **No live picture.** A spreadsheet is a snapshot. Inventory and orders change all day, so the team's view was only as current as the last update.

## Architecture and design

The application is written in Angular and TypeScript, talks to REST APIs, and covers five business areas: orders, inventory, catalogue, users and roles, and reporting.

- **Real-time dashboards.** Inventory and order monitoring dashboards, with charts built in Chart.js, give the operations team a live view instead of a spreadsheet snapshot.
- **Role-based access.** User accounts with roles and permissions control who can see and change what.

## Key technical challenges

### Replacing the spreadsheets

**Challenge.** Move the operations team off manual spreadsheet tracking for inventory and orders, onto an application they would actually rely on.

**Solution.** I designed and built the full UI for inventory oversight and order management, with real-time dashboards for inventory and order monitoring.

**Outcome.** The operations team adopted the dashboards as their primary daily tool, replacing spreadsheet tracking. Manual data-entry errors fell by 50% over the three months after launch.
