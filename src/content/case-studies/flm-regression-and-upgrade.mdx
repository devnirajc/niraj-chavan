## Context

The Field Logistics Module (FLM) is the Angular application Schlumberger, now SLB, uses to manage its global logistics inventory for field operations. More than 500 engineers in several regions use it. I worked on it as a UI developer from 2019 to 2021.

Two problems shaped this work:

- **A three-day regression cycle.** A full QA regression pass took three days.
- **An ageing framework.** FLM ran on Angular 8. Angular 11 shipped in November 2020, the same month Angular 8's long-term support ended.

## Results

- **Regression in hours, not days:** a full regression pass went from three days to under six hours, short enough to finish within a working day.
- **Coverage:** about 80% unit-test coverage with Jasmine and Karma, alongside a full WebDriver end-to-end suite reported through Allure.
- **A supported platform:** FLM moved from Angular 8 to Angular 11 with zero production downtime for its 500+ users.
